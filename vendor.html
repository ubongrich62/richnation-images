RichNation Mall, Vendor Terms & Conditions
This agreement governs your participation as a vendor on RichNation Mall, a division of RichNation Global Ltd. By applying to sell on RichNation Mall, you agree to be bound by these terms.
1. Eligibility & Application
1.1 You must provide accurate, truthful information in your vendor application, store name, owner name, contact details, and any documentation requested. False information is grounds for immediate rejection or removal.
1.2 Approval is at RichNation Mall's sole discretion. Approval is not guaranteed, and admin may decline an application without providing a reason.
1.3 You must be legally able to sell the products you list, this includes any required licenses, permits, or age restrictions for regulated goods.

2. Product Listings
2.1 Accuracy. Every listing must accurately describe the product, condition, specifications, materials, and true photos of the actual item (not stock photos misrepresenting what a customer will receive).
2.2 Pricing. You set your own prices, but pricing must be honest, no artificially inflating a price to then show a fake "discount."
2.3 Prohibited Items. You may not list: counterfeit or replica goods, stolen goods, weapons or ammunition, illegal drugs or drug paraphernalia, items that infringe another party's intellectual property, adult content, or any item prohibited under Nigerian law.
2.4 Stock Accuracy. Keep your stock levels updated. Repeatedly selling items you don't actually have in stock is a violation of this agreement.

2.5 Business Documentation. Before your store goes live, you must upload the following:
2.5.1 A valid means of identification belonging to the store owner (National ID, Driver's License, International Passport, or Voter's Card).
2.5.2 A recent passport photograph of the store owner.
2.5.3 Proof of address (a recent utility bill or bank statement in the owner's name).
2.5.4 Business Registration, your CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission) Certificate, or Business Name Registration if registered as a sole proprietorship.
2.5.5 Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum & Articles of Association, if you are a registered limited company.
2.5.6 Your Tax Identification Number (TIN).
2.5.7 Your Bank Verification Number (BVN) or a recent bank statement matching the account you'll receive payouts to.
2.5.8 Any category-specific license or permit required for what you sell, for example, NAFDAC registration for food, drinks, cosmetics, or health products; SON certification for electronics and regulated equipment; or any other regulatory approval your product category requires under Nigerian law.
2.5.9 A company profile or letterhead, where applicable.
2.5.10 Any additional documentation admin may reasonably request to verify your business.
RichNation Mall reviews every document as part of your application before approval, take your time to gather these properly rather than rush the process. Submitting false, forged, or expired documents is grounds for immediate and permanent removal, and admin reserves the right to request updated documents at any time during your time as a vendor.

3. Orders & Fulfillment
3.1 You must process and prepare orders for dispatch within the timeframe shown in your dashboard for each order.
3.2 Once an order is marked ready, RichNation Mall's dispatch/rider system (or your own agreed fulfillment method) takes over delivery.
3.3 Repeated failure to fulfill orders on time affects your Fulfillment Rate and overall standing (see Section 7).

4. Commission & Payments
4.1 RichNation Mall charges a commission on every sale, shown in your Payouts dashboard. This rate may be adjusted by admin with notice; your existing listings are not retroactively affected for completed sales.
4.2 Your net earnings (sale price minus commission) accumulate in your available balance, viewable anytime in your Payouts page.
4.3 Payouts are processed by admin to the bank details you provide. You are solely responsible for the accuracy of your bank details, RichNation Mall is not liable for funds sent to an incorrect account you provided.
4.4 RichNation Mall does not guarantee a fixed payout schedule beyond what is communicated to you directly; payouts are recorded and visible in your dashboard as they are made.

5. Returns, Refunds & Disputes
5.1 If a customer returns an item due to your error (wrong item, damaged on arrival, not as described), the cost of that return is your responsibility, and the sale amount may be reversed from your earnings.
5.2 If a customer returns an item for a reason unrelated to your listing (changed their mind, where our return policy allows it), standard marketplace return terms apply.
5.3 Disputes between you and a customer should first be handled directly through your Messages tab. Unresolved disputes may be escalated to RichNation Mall admin for a final decision.

6. Customer Communication
6.1 You must respond to customer questions and complaints professionally and in a reasonable timeframe.
6.2 You may not use customer contact information obtained through RichNation Mall for purposes outside of fulfilling their order (no unsolicited marketing, no directing them to buy from you outside the platform).

7. Performance Standards
7.1 Your Cancellation Rate and Order Defect Rate are tracked and shown on your Performance page. Staying at or below 5% on both keeps you in Good Standing.
7.2 Consistently exceeding these thresholds may result in: reduced visibility of your products, a formal warning from admin, temporary suspension, or in serious/repeated cases, removal from the platform.
7.3 RichNation Mall reviews performance holistically, a single unusual event will not automatically penalize you, but a sustained pattern will.

8. Suspension & Termination
8.1 RichNation Mall may suspend or terminate your vendor account for: violation of these terms, sustained poor performance, fraudulent activity, or conduct that harms customers or RichNation Mall's reputation.
8.2 You may close your store voluntarily at any time by contacting admin. Outstanding earnings owed to you will still be paid out according to Section 4.
8.3 Upon termination, your product listings are removed from the storefront. Historical order records are retained for legal and accounting purposes.

9. Intellectual Property
9.1 You retain ownership of your product photos, descriptions, and store branding, but grant RichNation Mall the right to display them on the platform for the purpose of selling your products.
9.2 You must not upload content you don't have the rights to use.

10. Limitation of Liability
10.1 RichNation Mall provides the marketplace platform but is not the manufacturer or owner of your products. Product quality, safety, and accuracy of description are your responsibility.
10.2 RichNation Mall's liability to you is limited to unpaid earnings genuinely owed under this agreement.

11. Changes to This Agreement
11.1 RichNation Mall may update these terms with notice through the Vendor Portal. Continued selling after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
