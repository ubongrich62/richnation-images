Welcome to RichNation. RichNation Global Ltd (RC 8998019) is a faith-centered digital media and education company, "Build People, Build Platforms, Create Millionaires." The Opportunity. We are raising ₦7,000,000 in partnership capital to stock, market, and scale RichNation Mall. Your investment is not locked to what the mall is today, it grows as the business grows.

Investment Structures. Within any package you choose: Monthly Profit Share (a share of profits, paid monthly), Equity Ownership (a permanent stake tied to RichNation's growth), or Fixed Return (a set annual rate for an agreed term). You may request to switch structures after a minimum 3-month holding period, subject to admin approval, this resets your payout timeline.

What Your Capital Funds. Product inventory, marketing and customer acquisition, warehouse/office space, logistics and delivery, technology, and staff and operations, so the mall can stock up, reach customers, and fulfil orders reliably.
