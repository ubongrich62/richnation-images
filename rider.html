A Direct Word From the Founder, Please Read
You are the last person our customer sees before they hold what they paid for. To them, you are RichNation. Customers have complained before about riders being rude, impatient, or rough with packages. This will not be taken lightly.

What I expect: wait patiently if a customer needs a moment. Answer questions calmly and politely, even the same one for the hundredth time. Carry every package the way you would carry something you paid for yourself.

If a customer treats you badly: do not argue back and do not respond anyhow. Stay professional, finish the delivery, and report it to your logistics manager or to management directly. We will handle it from there, and if you handled it the right way, we stand behind you.

Consequences that are not negotiable: confirmed rudeness, impatience, or careless handling follows our strike system, a formal strike on the first confirmed report, another on the second, and automatic suspension on the third. Running away with a customer's product, or anything close to theft, is not a strike matter. It is immediate termination, treated as theft.

This is written because I respect you enough to tell you exactly what is expected, before you sign, not after a mistake.
